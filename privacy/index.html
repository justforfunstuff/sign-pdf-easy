Sign PDF Easy Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Summary

Sign PDF Easy never sees your documents or signatures: everything you open, type, draw or sign stays on your computer. The only time the app uses the internet is to show the advertisement in the strip at the top of the window, which keeps the app free.

Sign PDF Easy ("the app") is published by the developer named on the app's Microsoft Store page ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information and the choices you have.

By downloading, installing or using Sign PDF Easy, you agree to this privacy policy. If you do not agree, do not download or use the app. You can uninstall it at any time.

Information that stays on your computer

We do not collect, upload, store or have access to any of the following. The app handles it only on your device.

  • PDF files you open: the app displays them and saves a signed copy only where you choose.
  • Text you type, dates and symbols: drawn onto the signed copy you save.
  • Signatures and initials you save: kept in the app's private storage on your computer so you can reuse them.
  • Text on scanned pages: read by the text recognition built into Windows to find blanks to fill. It is not saved.
  • Error details: if the app runs into an unexpected problem, it writes a log to %LOCALAPPDATA%\PdfSigner\crash.log on your computer.

The error log never leaves your computer unless you choose to send it to us when asking for help. It contains technical details about the error, which may include a file or folder name, but never the contents of your documents.

Advertising

The app shows one advertisement at a time in a strip at the top of the window, labelled "Ad". To do this it connects to the internet in these ways:

  • Ad list: the app downloads a small list of current ads from a server we control.
  • Ad images: the app downloads the banner images in that list, from the addresses the list gives.
  • Advertising partner: some ads are delivered by our advertising partners, through a small web view inside the ad strip.

When the app makes these connections, the servers involved, including our advertising partner's, may receive standard technical information: your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and Windows version, language, and the time of the request. Our advertising partner may use cookies or similar technologies inside the ad strip to show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Their use of this information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Advertising never receives your documents, file names, the text you type, or your signatures. When you click an ad, it opens in your usual web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Microsoft Store and Windows

Sign PDF Easy is distributed through the Microsoft Store and runs on Windows components made by Microsoft, including the Store, the PDF rendering and text recognition built into Windows, and the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime used for the ad strip. Microsoft may collect diagnostic, crash and usage data from these components according to your Windows privacy settings and the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Through Microsoft Partner Center, we receive summary reports such as install counts, ratings, reviews and anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you to us.

The "Rate it in the Microsoft Store" link opens the Store app, where Microsoft's privacy statement applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Where our advertising partner offers the setting, we turn off personalised ads for users the law protects as children.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing about you for us to look up, correct or delete. You stay in control of what is on your computer:

  • Saved signatures: delete them from the Signature or Initials menu, under "Delete a saved signature".
  • All app data: uninstalling the app removes its saved signatures and private storage. The error log can be deleted from %LOCALAPPDATA%\PdfSigner.
  • Ads: you can reset or limit ad tracking in Windows under Settings > Privacy & security > General. To opt out of interest-based advertising, turn off "Let apps show me personalized ads by using my advertising ID" in that Windows setting. You can also opt out with participating ad companies at optout.aboutads.info (United States) or youronlinechoices.eu (Europe).

If you live in a region with privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK) or the CCPA (California), you have rights to access, correct, delete and object to the use of your personal data. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it. For data held by our advertising partner or by Microsoft, contact them directly. For anything else, contact us through the support contact listed on the app's Microsoft Store page, and we will respond within 30 days.

Security and retention

Your documents and signatures are protected by keeping them off the internet entirely. Saved signatures sit in the app's private storage, which Windows keeps separate from other apps, and stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app. When you open a password-protected PDF and save a signed copy, the copy keeps the same password. Anyone who can sign in to your Windows account can use the app, so protect your account with a password or PIN.

The ad list and ad images are requested over encrypted HTTPS connections. We keep no records of these requests ourselves.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top and publish the new version at justforfunstuff.github.io/sign-pdf-easy/privacy and in the Microsoft Store. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will also mention it in the app's release notes in the Microsoft Store. Installing an update or continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
